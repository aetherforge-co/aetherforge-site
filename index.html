FAQ

// SHIPPING · RETURNS · ORDERING
// Ordering & Quotes
How do quotes work for custom prints?
Upload a model or describe what you need through the print shop's request form. We price it by material, layer height, and turnaround — usually back to you within a day. No account or commitment needed to get a number.
What file formats do you accept?
STL and STEP cover almost everything. If you're starting from a sketch or photo instead of a file, describe it in the request and we'll work out the rest with you before quoting.
Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?
If it hasn't started printing yet, yes — reply to your order confirmation and we'll adjust or cancel it. Once a job is on the bed, we can't stop it partway through.
// 3D Printing
What materials do you print in?
PLA, PETG, ASA, Nylon-CF, and resin, stocked in-house. Full specs and use cases are in the Material Library over in the print shop.
Do you print functional parts, or just prototypes?
Both. Rapid Prototyping is for quick iteration; Custom Enclosures and Printed Accessories are meant to be the actual part you use, not a placeholder for a "real" one later.
// Shipping
Where do orders ship from, and how fast?
Everything ships from Bay 4, Unit B. Typical turnaround is 24-48 hours from order to shipment for in-stock print items; custom jobs depend on the queue and we'll give you a specific estimate with your quote.
Do you ship internationally?
Domestic shipping only for now. International shipping is on the list as the print farm — and eventually the metal work line — grows.
// Returns & Warranty
What's your return policy?
If a printed part arrives with a defect — layer separation, warping, a dimension that's off from spec — we'll reprint or refund it, no questions asked. If you just changed your mind on a custom job, reach out and we'll figure out something fair; custom prints can't always go back on the shelf the way stocked items can.
What if my part arrives damaged in shipping?
Send a photo through the contact form within 7 days of delivery and we'll get a replacement out — no need to ship the damaged one back first.
// Metal Work (Coming Soon)
When will CNC hardware actually be available?
No fixed date yet — we're not shipping metal parts before we can hold the tolerances we'd put our name on. The Metal Work section has the current concept lineup and a "get notified" option for when that changes.
