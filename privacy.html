1. What We Collect

  • Order info: name, email, shipping address, and order contents — collected at checkout.
  • Payment info: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Quote / contact requests: whatever you enter in a "Request a Quote," "Notify Me," or contact form — typically name, email, and your message.
  • Submitted designs: if you send us a design/model file or create an account to store one, we keep it on file to quote and produce your order (see Terms, Section 4, for how it's used).
  • Chat messages: if you use the AETHER chat widget, your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response.

We don't use tracking cookies or browser local storage for advertising, and your cart isn't saved between visits — it only exists in your browser tab while you're shopping.
