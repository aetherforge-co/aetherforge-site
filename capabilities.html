What We Do

// CORE COMPETENCIES

Production-grade FDM in engineering polymers, run and inspected in-house. We are a polymer additive shop — we do not currently machine metal, and we will say so rather than quote work we cannot hold tolerance on.

  • Tooling, jigs and fixtures. Shop aids, alignment tools, assembly fixtures, soft jaws and locating blocks.
  • Obsolete and replacement parts. Reverse-engineered from a sample or a drawing where the original source is gone or lead times are unacceptable.
  • Organisational and support equipment. Trays, racks, mounts, housings, cable management, field-expedient hardware.
  • Prototypes and low-volume production. Iterative development through to short verified runs — up to 500 units per run.
  • Enclosures and protective housings. Printed to your dimensions in UV-stable or impact-resistant material as the application requires.

Quality

// HOW PARTS ARE CHECKED
  • Every part measured against spec before packing — not spot-checked at the end of a batch.
  • Non-conforming parts are reprinted at our cost. Layer separation, warping, or a dimension out of tolerance is on us, not on you.
  • Certificates of Conformance available on request for any run, stating material, lot and inspection result.
  • First article on request for production runs, before the balance is committed.
  • Material lot recorded per job, so a part can be traced back to the spool it came from.

We are not ISO 9001 or AS9100 certified. The above is a documented in-house process, not an accredited quality system. If your program requires a certified supplier, we are not the right shop yet — and we would rather tell you now.

What that means for your package

// SCOPE WE CAN ACCEPT TODAY
  • Non-controlled technical data only. We are not ITAR-registered, so we cannot receive or hold ITAR-controlled technical data. Please confirm export control status before sending drawings.
